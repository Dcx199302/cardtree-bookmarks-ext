CardTree Bookmarks

Privacy Policy  v1.0.0

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Overview

CardTree Bookmarks is a Chrome extension that replaces the default new tab page with a card-tree bookmark manager. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data Collection

CardTree Bookmarks does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API.

Permissions

  • bookmarks — Required to read, create, move, and delete your bookmarks.
  • storage — Used to save your preferences (theme, display settings, trash items) locally.
  • favicon — Used to display website icons next to bookmarks.
  • tabs — Used to open bookmarks in new tabs and track active tab state for visit counting.
  • sessions — Used to show recently closed tabs on the new tab page.

Data Storage

All data remains on your device. No data is sent to any server, API, or third-party service. If you uninstall the extension, all locally stored data is removed.

Third-Party Services

On Chrome, this extension does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

On Firefox, website favicons are fetched via Google's favicon service (https://www.google.com/s2/favicons). This means the domain names of your bookmarks are sent to Google when favicons are loaded. No other data is transmitted.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

概述

CardTree Bookmarks 是一款 Chrome 浏览器扩展，将默认新标签页替换为卡片树书签管理器。我们尊重您的隐私，致力于保护您的个人数据。

数据收集

CardTree Bookmarks 不会收集、传输或在外部服务器上存储任何个人数据。所有数据均通过 Chrome 内置的 chrome.storage.local API 存储在您的本地设备上。

权限说明

  • bookmarks（书签） — 用于读取、创建、移动和删除您的书签。
  • storage（存储） — 用于在本地保存您的偏好设置（主题、显示设置、回收站项目）。
  • favicon（图标） — 用于在书签旁显示网站图标。
  • tabs（标签页） — 用于在新标签页中打开书签，并追踪活动标签页状态以统计访问次数。
  • sessions（会话） — 用于在新标签页上显示最近关闭的标签页。

数据存储

所有数据均保存在您的设备上，不会发送到任何服务器、API 或第三方服务。卸载扩展后，所有本地存储的数据将被清除。

第三方服务

在 Chrome 上，本扩展不集成、不向任何第三方服务、分析平台或广告网络发送数据。

在 Firefox 上，网站图标通过 Google 的 favicon 服务（https://www.google.com/s2/favicons）获取。这意味着加载图标时，书签的域名会被发送至 Google。除此之外不会传输任何其他数据。

政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更将在此页面上以更新日期的形式体现。

联系方式
